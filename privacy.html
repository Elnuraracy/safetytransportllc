1. Information We Collect Through Our Website

We do not currently collect any personal information through this website. Our site does not include contact forms, account logins, quote request forms, or newsletter sign-ups, and we do not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or advertising technology to collect visitor data at this time. If this changes in the future — for example, if we add a contact form or quote request tool — this Policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and how it is used before that feature goes live.

2. Information We Collect Through Business Operations

Separately from our website, we may collect personal information directly from drivers, vendors, repair shops, legal counsel, and business partners in the ordinary course of operating a commercial trucking business. This includes information provided to us by phone, SMS, and email, such as:

  • Name and company/vendor name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Business address
  • The content of SMS messages, emails, and calls exchanged with us regarding dispatch, repairs, or compliance matters
  • Information related to freight, dispatch, vehicle repair, and DOT/FMCSA compliance coordination

3. How We Use Information

  • To coordinate freight dispatch, driver assignments, and delivery scheduling
  • To communicate with repair shops, vendors, drivers, and legal counsel about active business matters (e.g., repair status, appointment confirmations, inspection follow-up)
  • To send and receive SMS messages related to ongoing business coordination
  • To maintain records required under DOT/FMCSA regulations
  • To respond to inquiries from drivers, vendors, contractors, and business partners

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information or SMS opt-in/consent data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates.

We may share information only with:

  • Service providers necessary to operate our business (e.g., dispatch, fleet management, and communications platforms)
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law (e.g., DOT, FMCSA, state transportation agencies)
  • Legal counsel retained on our behalf for compliance or litigation matters

5. SMS Terms of Service

By opting into SMS from a web form or other medium, you are agreeing to receive SMS messages from Safety Transport LLC. This includes SMS messages for conversations (external) related to dispatch coordination, repair and vendor communication, appointment confirmations, and compliance-related follow-up.

  • Messaging frequency may vary.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To opt out at any time, text STOP.
  • For assistance, text HELP or visit safetytransportllc.com.

6. How We Protect Information

We maintain reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, consistent with FMCSA recordkeeping requirements.

7. How Long We Retain Information

We retain information only as long as necessary for business and regulatory compliance purposes.

8. Your Choices & Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you by contacting us using the details in Section 12. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message.
