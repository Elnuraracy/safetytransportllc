About Safety Transport LLC

Built by a safety team first — dispatch came second.

Safety Transport LLC is based in Joliet, Illinois, and works with CDL drivers and truck owners to run freight for Amazon Relay. The company was started by people with a compliance and safety background, so the systems for keeping trucks and drivers legal came before anything else.

That's still how the company runs today: dispatch plans the work, and the safety team keeps every truck and driver file in order behind it.
